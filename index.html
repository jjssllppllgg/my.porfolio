Healthcare Experience with a Patient-First Mindset

I am a detail-oriented Healthcare Virtual Assistant with hands-on experience supporting optometry and contact lens operations. My work has involved patient communication, insurance and vision benefit verification, order coordination, billing support, and maintaining clear patient documentation.

My previous experience in medical billing and member services also helped me understand the importance of reviewing account details carefully and explaining information in a way patients can understand.
